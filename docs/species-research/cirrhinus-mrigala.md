## 3. Identification

**Key Features** (FishBase, FAO, ITIS):
- Body laterally compressed, fusiform
- Mouth inferior, fringed lips
- Two pairs of barbels (maxillary and rostral)
- Dorsal fin with 12-14 branched rays
- Scales cycloid, lateral line complete with 40-44 scales
- Pharyngeal teeth in 3 rows (5.4.2 / 2.4.5)

**Diagnostic Characteristics** (FishBase, FAO):
- Body depth 3.0-3.5 times in standard length
- Head length 3.5-4.0 times in standard length
- Eye diameter 4.5-5.5 times in head length
- Snout blunt, projecting beyond mouth
- Two pairs of barbels (rostral and maxillary)

**Distinguishing Features** (FishBase, FAO):
- Distinguished from other Cirrhinus species by: two pairs of barbels, body proportions, scale counts
- Similar to Cirrhinus cirrhosus (Mrigal carp) but distinct species

**Source**: FishBase, FAO, ITIS

## 5. Biology

**Habitat** (FishBase, FAO, IUCN):
- Freshwater, benthopelagic
- Rivers, lakes, ponds, reservoirs
- Bottom feeder
- Temperature range: 18-38°C (optimal 25-32°C)
- Depth range: 1-10 m (FishBase)

**Behavior** (FishBase, FAO):
- Active during day
- Forms schools
- Bottom feeder
- Migrates laterally to floodplains during monsoon

**Reproduction** (FishBase, FAO, IUCN):
- Sexual maturity: 1-2 years
- Spawning season: Monsoon (June-August)
- Spawning temperature: 24-31°C
- Fecundity: 100,000-800,000 eggs/kg body weight
- Spawning substrate: Marginal vegetation in flooded areas
- No parental care
- Migration: Potamodromous, lateral to floodplains during monsoon

**Diet** (FishBase, FAO, IUCN):
- Feeding type: Detritivore
- Feeds on detritus, algae, organic matter from bottom
- Bottom feeder, grazes on periphyton
- Juveniles: zooplankton, phytoplankton
