## 3. Identification

**Key Features** (FishBase, FAO, Wikipedia):
- Body laterally compressed, deep
- Head very large, broad
- Mouth wide, upturned with prominent protruding lower jaw
- Upper lip absent, lower lip very thick
- No barbels
- Eyes large, visible from underside of head
- Scales large, cycloid
- Lateral line complete

**Diagnostic Characteristics** (FishBase, FAO):
- Body depth 2.0-2.5 times in standard length
- Head length 2.5-3.0 times in standard length
- Eye diameter 4.0-5.0 times in head length
- Mouth wide, upturned with prominent protruding lower jaw
- Upper lip absent, lower lip very thick and fleshy
- No barbels
- Scales large, cycloid, lateral line complete

**Distinguishing Features** (FishBase, FAO):
- Distinguished from other Indian major carps by: very large head, upturned mouth with protruding lower jaw, no barbels
- Formerly placed in monotypic genus Catla, now synonymized with Labeo/Gibelion

**Source**: FishBase, FAO, Wikipedia (with IUCN/FishBase sources)

## 5. Biology

**Habitat** (FishBase, FAO, IUCN):
- Freshwater, pelagic to benthopelagic
- Rivers, lakes, reservoirs, ponds
- Surface and midwater dweller
- Temperature range: 18-38°C (optimal 25-32°C)
- Depth range: 0-10 m (FishBase)

**Behavior** (FishBase, FAO):
- Active during day
- Forms schools
- Surface and midwater feeder
- Migrates upstream for spawning during monsoon

**Reproduction** (FishBase, FAO, IUCN):
- Sexual maturity: 2 years (average)
- Spawning season: Monsoon (June-August)
- Spawning temperature: 24-32°C
- Fecundity: 150,000-1,000,000 eggs/kg body weight
- Spawning substrate: Marginal vegetation in flooded areas
- No parental care
- Migration: Potamodromous, lateral to floodplains during monsoon

**Diet** (FishBase, FAO, Wikipedia):
- Feeding type: Planktivore
- Adults: Zooplankton (large gill rakers)
- Juveniles: Zooplankton and phytoplankton
- Surface and midwater feeder
- Filter feeds using large gill rakers

**Source Agreement**: FishBase, FAO, IUCN agree on biological parameters
