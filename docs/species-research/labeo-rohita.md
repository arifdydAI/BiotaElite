## 3. Identification

**Key Features** (FishBase, FAO):
- Body laterally compressed, fusiform
- Mouth inferior, fringed lips
- No barbels
- Dorsal fin with 12-14 branched rays
- Pharyngeal teeth in 3 rows (5.4.2 / 2.4.5)
- Scales cycloid, lateral line complete with 40-44 scales

**Diagnostic Characteristics** (FishBase, FAO):
- Body depth 3.0-3.5 times in standard length
- Head length 3.5-4.0 times in standard length
- Eye diameter 4.5-5.5 times in head length
- Snout blunt, projecting beyond mouth
- Lower jaw with a sharp cutting edge

**Distinguishing Features** (FishBase, FAO):
- Distinguished from other Labeo species by: inferior mouth with fringed lips, no barbels, dorsal fin inserted slightly ahead of pelvic fins
- Similar to Labeo calbasu but differs in scale count and body proportions

**Source**: FishBase (Labeo rohita summary), FAO Species Fact Sheet

## 5. Biology

**Habitat** (FishBase, FAO, IUCN):
- Freshwater, benthopelagic
- Rivers, lakes, ponds, reservoirs
- Prefers slow-moving or standing waters
- Temperature range: 14-38°C (optimal 25-30°C)
- Depth range: 0-10 m (FishBase)

**Behavior** (FishBase, FAO):
- Active during day
- Forms schools
- Bottom and column feeder
- Migrates upstream for spawning during monsoon

**Reproduction** (FishBase, FAO, IUCN):
- Sexual maturity: 1-2 years
- Spawning season: Monsoon (June-August)
- Spawning temperature: 22-31°C
- Fecundity: 200,000-2,000,000 eggs/kg body weight
- Spawning substrate: Gravel/sand in flowing water
- No parental care
- Migration: Potamodromous, upstream for spawning during monsoon floods (up to 100 km)

**Diet** (FishBase):
- Herbivore (adults)
- Feeds on phytoplankton, algae, aquatic plants, detritus
- Juveniles: zooplankton, phytoplankton
- Feeding type: Herbivore

**Source Agreement**: FishBase and FAO agree on biological parameters
