About us

Who we are

BiotaElite started as a simple idea: make quality zoology resources free and accessible to everyone.

Our mission

Open science for curious minds

We believe the animal world should be studied and shared openly. BiotaElite brings together species assets, research projects, theses, news and articles so that students, educators and enthusiasts can learn from each other regardless of institution or budget.

Everything on this site is community-contributed and reviewed by a volunteer editorial team of educators and researchers.

🦁

Our values

  • ✓ Open and free access to knowledge
  • ✓ Ethical and humane treatment of animals
  • ✓ Accurate, evidence-based science
  • ✓ Inclusive community of contributors
